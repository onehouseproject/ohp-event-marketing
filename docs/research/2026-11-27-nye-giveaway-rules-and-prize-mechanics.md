## December 4 drawing

Alabama's lottery test looks for a prize, chance and consideration. In *Pepsi Cola Bottling Co. v. Coca-Cola*, the Alabama Supreme Court upheld a promotion with a genuinely accessible and advertised free entry route. The organizer's incidental sales benefit did not supply consideration. A single public form asking for name and email to run the drawing fits that reasoning. The review found no controlling Alabama decision on collecting a phone number solely for winner contact or conditioning entry on permission to market to that number. Use email for administration and make any marketing signup optional. [Alabama Code § 13A-12-20](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/code-of-alabama?chapter=12&section=13A-12-20&title=13A) · [*Pepsi Cola*, 534 So. 2d 295](https://law.justia.com/cases/alabama/supreme-court/1988/534-so-2d-295-1.html)

The prototype specifies one package, one entry per person, age 21+, a December 4 drawing and a refund for a winner who already booked. Its rules control opens placeholder copy. Its form requires mobile and email, asks for calls and texts as a condition of entry and does not send entries. For #21, replace the placeholder with a real rules link, take name and email, and enforce opening and closing times on the server. Keep the free URL available to buyers and nonbuyers even if the site suppresses repeat pop-ups. Record accepted entries separately from contact marketing status so the sponsor can verify the pool and draw. [Prototype terms](https://github.com/onehouseproject/ohp-event-marketing/blob/nye-giveaway-prototypes/app/(nye)/event.ts#L68-L87) · [Prototype form](https://github.com/onehouseproject/ohp-event-marketing/blob/nye-giveaway-prototypes/app/(nye)/_components/giveaway/GiveawayForm.tsx) · [Integration issue #21](https://github.com/onehouseproject/ohp-event-marketing/issues/21)

## Consent and follow-up

Send an entry confirmation and winner or alternate notices by email. Keep sales copy out of those messages. If OHP wants to send ticket reminders or future event news, use a separate optional, unchecked email signup. Preserve existing unsubscribes and use a valid postal address, clear unsubscribe mechanism and timely suppression for commercial email. Test delivery to a new entrant and to an existing contact who previously unsubscribed from marketing. [FTC CAN-SPAM guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)

The FCC's rule requires prior express consent for covered automated, artificial-voice or prerecorded telemarketing to the specified numbers when the outreach is by or on behalf of a tax-exempt nonprofit. Carrier and provider restrictions apply separately. Twilio expressly rejects A2P giveaway promotion, entry facilitation and prize notifications. HighLevel lists paid sweepstakes in its prohibited categories and has broader gambling restrictions under LC Phone. Approval for OHP's sender and this traffic was unverified. Use email for this drawing. For a later permitted SMS program, match the registered brand and message purpose, use optional unchecked opt-in by purpose, disclose frequency, rates, STOP and HELP, link the privacy notice and SMS terms, and record opt-in wording, time and source. [47 CFR § 64.1200(a)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200) · [Twilio giveaway policy](https://www.twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30945) · [HighLevel prohibited categories](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001219617-forbidden-message-categories-for-sms-and-mms-in-the-us-and-canada) · [LC Phone policy](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001213941-lc-messaging-policy) · [HighLevel registration guidance](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000004539-a2p-campaign-registration-step-by-step-guide-and-faqs)

## December 31 tickets and kitchen design prize

On November 27 the live event page advertised a $6,000 kitchen design package, listed prize eligibility in every paid tier and showed 3×, 2× and 1× starting chips. The copy did not establish how the winner would be chosen. Huntsville Casino Parties describes chips-to-drawing-tickets as its standard setup. Its public description does not establish OHP's event-specific arrangements. Givebutter called the prize a “Free Kitchen Remodel Giveaway,” while the event page described design services only. [Live event page](https://events.onehouseproject.com/nye) · [Givebutter campaign](https://givebutter.com/onehousenye) · [Dealer's standard setup](https://www.huntsvillecasinoparties.com/how-it-works)

In *Youngblood v. Bailey*, the Alabama Supreme Court held that a paid chamber event ticket bundling admission and a random chance at a car was an illegal lottery. Equal odds among paid guests leave the admission requirement in place. A donated service is a prize of value, and chips with no cash value can still affect the value of the prize opportunity if they earn entries. *Yellow-Stone Kit v. State* illustrates the available structure. Its free drawing was genuinely independent of paid seating and absent entrants could win. [*Youngblood*, 459 So. 2d 855](https://law.justia.com/cases/alabama/supreme-court/1984/459-so-2d-855-1.html) · [*Yellow-Stone Kit*, 88 Ala. 196](https://openjurist.org/88/ala/196/yellow-stone-kit-v-state) · [Alabama Code § 13A-12-20](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/code-of-alabama?chapter=12&section=13A-12-20&title=13A)

Confirm the dealer's actual setup with Alabama counsel. Keep starting stacks only as an entertainment feature, with free resets and no buying, transferring or redeeming chips. If keeping the design prize, use a separate public entry form and rules, give every eligible entrant equal odds and let the winner claim remotely. Remove the paid-tier prize eligibility line and correct the Givebutter headline. If tickets already sold with advertised prize eligibility, review whether buyers need notice or a refund option. Givebutter's guidelines list casino games and sweepstakes broadly. Confirm the revised admission-only campaign's eligibility with Givebutter before directing paid traffic to it. [Givebutter terms and community guidelines](https://givebutter.com/terms)

For a non-wager prize awarded in 2026, the general Form 1099-MISC threshold is $2,000. Confirm which organization awards and reports any retained $6,000 design prize, its fair market value and who collects the winner's W-9. IRS Publication 3079 addresses tax treatment of gaming by exempt organizations. [IRS 2026 reporting guidance](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p1099) · [IRS Publication 3079](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p3079.pdf)

## Meta ads and privacy

Start the 21+ fundraiser ad after the landing page and checkout reflect the revised mechanics. Meta's gambling policy lists sweepstakes and defines covered online gambling by monetary value on both the entry and prize sides. The proposed free drawing has a reasonable basis to run without gambling authorization. Actual classification is Meta's decision. Send a separate giveaway ad directly to the free form and rules, without likes, shares or tags as entry steps or incentives. Use ad review if flagged. [Meta gambling ad policy](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/gambling-games/) · [Facebook promotion terms](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/pages_groups_events) · [Instagram promotion guidelines](https://help.instagram.com/179379842258600)

The posted notice already describes Meta measurement and retargeting, Givebutter's separate tags and opt-out choices. Revise the giveaway paragraph to describe the fields and purposes that ship. Suggested wording for the email-only form follows. If an optional marketing signup ships, disclose its use separately. Count entries and campaign source in HighLevel for launch and defer Meta Lead tracking and Conversions API for giveaway activity. Align #21 and #24 to that decision if adopted. [Posted privacy notice](https://events.onehouseproject.com/privacy) · [Meta Business Tools Terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/technology_terms) · [Conversions API issue #24](https://github.com/onehouseproject/ohp-event-marketing/issues/24)

> To enter, we ask for your name and email. We use this information to administer the drawing, confirm your entry and contact you if selected. We store entries in HighLevel, our contact manager.

The event Pixel's JavaScript checks Global Privacy Control, but its fallback image can send a page view with JavaScript disabled when a Pixel ID is configured. Remove the fallback or enforce the preference server-side. Test the OHP Pixel and distinguish it from Givebutter's own checkout tags. Keep the event tracker and hardship signals separate by leaving housing-assistance pages out of event tracking and reviewing any separate tracking on those pages against Meta's sensitive-data terms. [Pixel source](https://github.com/onehouseproject/ohp-event-marketing/blob/211f66bc0818ed09dc74a2e2461460e17313b8c1/app/(nye)/_components/MetaPixel.tsx#L7-L35) · [Meta Business Tools Terms §§ 1(h) and 3](https://www.facebook.com/legal/technology_terms)

Alabama HB 351, Act 2026-552, takes effect May 1, 2027. Its exemption for qualifying nonprofits with fewer than 100 employees depends on whether the organization sells personal data. Coverage also requires Alabama nexus plus either personal data of more than 25,000 Alabama consumers, excluding payment-completion-only data, or more than 25% of gross revenue from personal data sales. A noncash exchange can qualify as a sale if the controller receives a material benefit and the recipient's subsequent uses are unrestricted. The law excludes disclosures to processors acting on the controller's behalf, for analytics, and for marketing solely to the controller. Meta's terms also contemplate its own uses of event data, including personalization. Before May 1, verify OHP's volumes and the actual purposes, terms and settings for each browser, checkout and server-side data flow before relying on the exemption. [Enrolled Act §§ 2–4 and 12](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/HB351-enr.pdf) · [Meta Business Tools Terms § 2](https://www.facebook.com/legal/technology_terms)
